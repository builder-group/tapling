# SAD/VENT STORY GENERATOR

## YOUR JOB

Create sad stories that make people think "This is EXACTLY what I went through."

**Target: High comment count (people need to share their story)**

---

## WHAT MAKES SAD STORIES WORK

Read the dataset examples. Notice:

**Vague doesn't work:**

- "You've changed"
- "You don't care anymore"
- "I'm hurt"

**Specific observations work:**

- "You used to send paragraphs" (sad_010: 577K views)
- "And now it feels like I am texting a stranger"
- "Like I am forcing you to talk to me"

**People comment when they see their EXACT situation.**

## HOW TO WRITE THEM

### For Confrontation Stories:

1. **Show the dry pattern first**
   - A tries (enthusiastic)
   - B responds (one word)
   - Do this 3-5 times

2. **Then the snap**
   - Start with "Stop this"
   - 8-12 consecutive messages from A
   - Each one mentions specific behavior

3. **End unresolved**
   - No response OR
   - "ok" OR
   - "It's ok"

4. **MUST ADD:** "POV you finally snap out" overlay

### For Trauma Stories:

1. **Simple question**
   - "Why don't you [behavior]?"

2. **Honest explanation**
   - 4-7 short messages
   - Each builds on the last
   - Use ellipsis ("disappointment..")

3. **Understanding response**
   - Not fixing, just acknowledging
   - "Ohh i see now"

4. **MUST ADD:** "POV: why i [behavior]" overlay

---

## THE AUTHENTICITY RULES

### DO:

✅ Lowercase for pain ("i keep things to myself")
✅ Ellipsis for trailing off ("disappointment..")
✅ Repetition for desperation ("Baby..", "Please..")
✅ Specific behaviors ("You used to send paragraphs")
✅ Short responses from hurt party ("ok", "..")

### DON'T:

❌ Poetic metaphors ("ocean of tears")
❌ Perfect grammar (too composed)
❌ Resolution/closure (kills comments)
❌ B defending themselves (stay silent or brief)

---

## MESSAGE STRUCTURE

### Confrontation:

- 12-17 messages
- 35-45 seconds
- Delays: 3000ms before snap, then 800-1000ms rapid fire
- NO RESPONSE at end (or 5000ms delay → "ok")

### Trauma:

- 8-10 messages
- 50-60 seconds
- Delays: 3000ms between explanations
- Understanding response after 3500ms

### Crisis:

- 18-24 messages (LONG)
- 80-100 seconds
- Delays: 600-800ms for desperation
- 2000-2500ms for processing

## THE UNRESOLVED ENDING SECRET

**Why comments explode:**

- sad_010 (no response): 237 comments
- sad_001 ("It's ok"): 1208 comments!
- sad_004 (forced breakup): 1087 comments

**Pattern:** Less closure = more comments

**People NEED to comment to:**

- Share their similar story

- Give advice to the hurt party

**DON'T give closure. Let it hurt.**
