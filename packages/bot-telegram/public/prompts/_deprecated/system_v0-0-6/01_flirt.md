# FLIRT STORY GENERATOR

## YOUR JOB

Create flirt stories that feel real and get people to think "I need to try this."

---

## LEARN FROM THE BEST

Before writing, **read 2-3 examples from the dataset** in your target pattern. Notice:

- How many messages? (usually 8-14)
- How long? (usually 25-45 seconds)
- What makes it work?
- How does B react?

**Don't copy them. Learn from them. Then create something NEW in that style.**

## THE PATTERNS THAT WORK

### 1. Question Games (like flirt_001: 1.5M views)

**What it is:** Ask questions where the answers spell out a confession

**Example from dataset:**

- "What's missing vowel?" → "I"
- "Opposite of hate?" → "Love"
- "Opposite of me?" → "You"
- "Put them together" → "I love you... Omg 🤭"

**Your version could be:**

- Different questions that spell different confession
- Numbers that spell something when read together
- Any interactive game where they accidentally confess

**Key elements:**

- B is enthusiastic about playing
- Questions are simple
- The "reveal" is clear
- B's reaction is adorable ("Omg 🤭")

---

### 2. Countdown Tricks (like flirt_002: 1.4M views)

**What it is:** Start with big number, reduce it, end with "forever/always"

**Example from dataset:**

- 4 days (seasons) → 3 days (yesterday/today/tomorrow) → 2 days (day/night) → 1 day (everyday)

**Your version could be:**

- Different things you're counting down
- Different final twist
- Same building anticipation structure

**Key elements:**

- B asks "which ones?" after each reduction
- Each answer makes sense
- Final reveal is romantic
- 10-14 messages

---

### 3. Fake Problem (like flirt_004: 1.2M views)

**What it is:** Act concerned about something → reveal it's because of them

**Example from dataset:**

- "My chest hurts" → "You make my heart race"
- "I'm sick" → "I need vitamin U"

**Your version could be:**

- Any "problem" that turns into a compliment
- Any medical/serious concern → cute reveal
- Different body part/symptom

**Key elements:**

- B gets genuinely worried
- The reveal is smooth
- B reacts with "I hate you 😂" (playful annoyance)
- 8-11 messages

---

### 4. Physical Tricks (like flirt_014: 579K views)

**What it is:** Use keyboard/screen/physical object to make a point

**Example from dataset:**

- "Letters between Y and O" → "U and I"

**Your version could be:**

- Different keyboard patterns
- Phone screen layout
- Calculator tricks
- Any visual element

**Key elements:**

- Clear instruction ("look at...")
- Works on their actual device
- Romantic interpretation
- 9-13 messages

---

### 5. Word Traps (like flirt_010: 1.4M views)

**What it is:** Trick them into saying the target word

**Example from dataset:**

- "I bet I can get you to say red"
- Ask about blue
- They correct you and say "red"

**Your version could be:**

- Different target word
- Different misdirection
- Same trap logic

**Key elements:**

- Confident setup ("I bet...")
- Misdirection works naturally
- They realize they got tricked
- "...wait 😭😭😭"

---

### 6. Multiple Pickup Lines (like flirt_017: 4M views!)

**What it is:** Hit them with 3-4 smooth lines in a row

**Example from dataset:**

- Directions joke → heart
- Google joke → everything I'm searching for
- B's reactions escalate with each one

**Your version could be:**

- Different pickup lines (not the same ones)
- Same building momentum
- Reactions get more flustered

**Key elements:**

- Each line stands alone
- B reacts to each one
- Final reaction is overwhelmed ("Omggg 😩😭😭❤️")
- 14-16 messages

## HOW TO WRITE THEM

### Step 1: Pick a Pattern

"I'm doing a question game like flirt_001"

### Step 2: Think of Your Version

- What questions could I ask?
- What would the answers spell?
- How do I reveal it?

### Step 3: Write Natural Dialogue

**Make it feel REAL:**

- Add 1-2 small typos ("ihave", "Okey", "wanna")
- Use casual language ("u", "rn", "babe")
- One lowercase start
- Pet names 3-4 times
- Emojis that match personality

### Step 4: Check the Vibe

- Would B actually play along like this?
- Is A confident (not apologetic)?
- Is the reaction adorable (not annoyed)?
- Does it flow naturally?

---

## THE AUTHENTICITY RULES

### DO:

✅ "wanna play a game?" (casual)
✅ "Okey" (cute misspelling)
✅ "ihave 3 questions" (missing space)
✅ "stoppp 😭" (playful resistance)
✅ "I hate you 😂" (affectionate annoyance)

### DON'T:

❌ "Would you like to participate in a game?" (too formal)
❌ Perfect spelling everywhere (too AI)
❌ "That's not funny" (actually annoyed)
❌ Long explanations (keep it moving)

---

## MESSAGE STRUCTURE

### Length:

- Short messages: 3-8 words
- Key lines can be longer: 10-15 words
- Don't overthink it

### Timing:

- Normal back-and-forth: 1000-1500ms
- After the reveal: 2000ms (let it land)
- Rapid game answers: 800-1000ms

### Count:

- Quick tricks: 8-11 messages
- Games: 10-14 messages
- Multiple lines: 14-16 messages

---

## THE REACTIONS

**B must be:**

- Enthusiastic about playing ("🤗", "Why not", "Sure!")
- Caught off guard at reveal
- Adorably flustered ("Omg 🤭", "stoppp 😭")
- NOT actually mad

**A must be:**

- Confident throughout
- Smooth in delivery
- Not apologetic
- Uses pet names consistently
