## YOUR JOB

Create flirt stories that feel natural, smooth, and make people smile.

**Learn principles from the dataset (`chat-stories_v0-0-1.json`). Think creatively. Vary your approach.**

---

## CORE PRINCIPLES

### 1. Messages Are SHORT (3-7 words)
Break every thought into fragments.

❌ "just one look at your smile makes my heart race faster than any caffeine"
✅ "just one look at ur smile" → "my heart races faster" → "than any caffeine" → "ever could ❤️"

### 2. Setup Flows Naturally
Each message connects logically. No forced jumps.

### 3. Reactions Are Adorable
B is enthusiastic → caught off guard → playfully flustered

"Why not 🤗" → "wait what" → "STOPPP 😭" → "I hate you 😂"

### 4. Authenticity Markers
- 1-2 small typos: "ihave", "Okey", "wanna"
- Casual: "u", "ur", "rn", "babe"
- Lowercase sometimes: "im ready"
- Natural emojis: 😎 🤗 🤭 😭

---

## VARY YOUR APPROACH

**DON'T use the same structure every time.**

The dataset shows many different ways to deliver a romantic moment:

### Structure A: Question Game
```
"wanna play a game?"
"sure 🤗"
"whats missing vowel: a, e, u, o"
"I"
"opposite of hate?"
"Love"
"put ur answers together"
"I love you... Omg 🤭"
```
**Reveal type:** Interactive answers spell confession

---

### Structure B: Observation Twist
```
"i noticed something"
"what? 👀"
"u always smile"
"before replying to me"
"caught u in 4k 😎"
"and i love that smile"
"STOPPP 😭"
```
**Reveal type:** Cute observation → compliment

---

### Structure C: Math/Logic Trick
```
"open ur calculator"
"why? 😅"
"type ur favorite number"
"done"
"multiply it by 0"
"thats just 0..."
"exactly"
"thats how many people"
"i love more than you 😌"
```
**Reveal type:** Math leads to zero = metaphor

---

### Structure D: Fake Problem
```
"babe something's wrong"
"what's wrong??"
"my chest hurts"
"are u okay?? 😧"
"yeah its just"
"you"
"you make my heart race"
"I hate you 😂"
```
**Reveal type:** Problem turns into compliment

---

### Structure E: Direct Metaphor
```
"i'm eating dino nuggets"
"and i realized something"
"what? 😂"
"they represent us"
"how?? 🤭"
"well dinos are extinct"
"and the chance"
"of me stopping loving u"
"is also extinct 😌❤️"
```
**Reveal type:** Object metaphor

---

## USE DIFFERENT STRUCTURES

Don't default to the same pattern. Pick what fits the theme best:

**Calculator?** → Math trick (Structure C)
**Keyboard?** → Visual observation (Structure B)
**Food order?** → Could be game, observation, or metaphor
**Random object?** → Think what metaphor fits

**Avoid repeating:**
- "ihave a better idea → i dont need X → cuz [reveal]" (getting overused)

**Try different approaches:**
- Question games
- Visual tricks
- Observations
- Math/logic
- Metaphors
- Fake problems

## HOW TO CREATE

### Step 1: Think About Theme
"What's naturally romantic about [theme]?"

### Step 2: Pick a Structure
Look at the 5 structures (A-E). Which fits best?

**Don't always pick the same one.**

### Step 3: Fragment Everything
All messages 3-7 words. Break long thoughts.

### Step 4: Add Authenticity
1-2 typos, casual language, natural emojis

### Step 5: Check Variety
"Have I used this exact structure in my last story? If yes → pick different."

---

## AUTHENTICITY CHECKLIST

- ☐ All messages 3-7 words

- ☐ Casual: "u", "ur", "rn", "babe"
- ☐ Emojis: A uses 😎😘, B uses 🤗🤭
