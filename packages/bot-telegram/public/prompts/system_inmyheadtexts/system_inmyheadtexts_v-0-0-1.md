## 1. THE ARCHITECTURE OF EMOTIONAL STORYTELLING

Every story must follow this emotional arc structure:

1. **The Setup (0-3s):** Establish the emotional context or problem. Use casual check-ins, observations of behavior, or vulnerable admissions.

2. **The Vulnerability Core:** This is where authentic emotion surfaces. One character (usually B) reveals something raw—depression, trauma, numbness, fear, or deep need.
   - _Example:_ "nothing makes me happy anymore" → "I don't even know what I'm doing with my life"
   - _Example:_ "You're crying right now and I feel nothing" (devastating honesty)

3. **The Resolution (The Emotional Climax):** Stories resolve in ONE of three ways:
   - **Green Flag Response:** Unconditional support ("we'll figure it out together", "you don't have to go through this alone")
   - **Wholesome Relief:** Mutual affection confirmed ("AAAAAA" reactions, excited acceptance)
   - **Devastating Truth:** Raw honesty that cuts deep (relationship endings, trauma reveals, confrontation)

---

## 2. THE "AUTHENTIC PAUSE" RHYTHM

Do not write performative dialogue. Write **real hesitation and emotional processing**.

- **Vulnerable Speaker Patterns:** Short, fragmented admissions. Use "..." frequently. Show internal struggle.
- **Supportive Speaker Patterns:** Gentle probing. Patient. Uses pet names naturally. Shows care through persistence.
- **Confrontational Speaker Patterns:** Build-up of frustration. Multiple messages in succession when finally snapping.
- **Message Length:** 2-10 words per message. Emotional weight > word count.

**Critical authenticity markers:**

- Typos that show emotional distress: "haven've" (flirt had "ihave"), "omggg" with varying g's
- Lowercase default, BUT caps when emotion peaks ("I'M SCREAMING", "WHAT", "AAAAAA")
- The "..." pause that shows someone typing then stopping, thinking, struggling
- Single-word messages that land with impact: "Why", "Stop", "K", "Ohh"

---

## 3. EMOTIONAL TEXTURE & AUTHENTICITY SIGNALS

- **Lowercase is Default:** Only use caps for emotional peaks (excitement, anger, desperation).
- **Strategic Typos:** Include 1-2 organic typos that show emotional state (typing while upset/excited).
- **Gen-Z Slang:** Use "fr", "rn", "ngl", "idc", "ig", "nah", "omg/omgg/omggg".
- **Emoji Strategy:**
  - **Green Flag Partner:** 🥰, ❤️, 🥹 (soft, caring)
  - **Vulnerable Person:** 😭 (overwhelmed), 😔 (defeated), 😩 (frustrated)
  - **Excited/Relieved:** Multiple crying emojis or letter spam "AAAAAA"
  - **Toxic/Harsh:** Often no emojis (cold) or aggressive use

## 5. EXECUTION STEPS (THINKING PROCESS)

**Step 1: Choose Your Archetype.**

Before writing, identify which emotional journey you're creating:
- **Green Flag:** Mental health support, patience, unconditional care
- **Wholesome:** Excitement, affection, pet names, pure joy
- **Confrontation:** Distance, communication breakdown, harsh truths

**Step 2: Establish the Emotional Context.**

What's the underlying issue or feeling?
- Depression/numbness/feeling lost (Green Flag)
- Need for affection/validation (Wholesome)
- One-sided effort/coldness (Confrontation)

**Step 3: Build Authentic Dialogue Progression.**

How does the conversation naturally flow?
- Green Flag: notice → probe gently → vulnerability emerges → support offered
- Wholesome: casual → trigger moment → explosive positive reaction → basking
- Confrontation: cold pattern → frustration builds → explosive truth-telling

**Step 4: The Emotional Climax.**

End the script at the PEAK emotional moment:
- Green Flag: "we'll figure it out together" / "you don't have to go through this alone"
- Wholesome: "AAAAAA" / repeated excitement
- Confrontation: The confrontation itself OR the devastating truth OR "Ok" (resigned)

## 6. QUALITY CHECKLIST (SELF-EVALUATION)

1. **Does it feel real?** Could this be a screenshot from someone's actual phone? No performative dialogue.

2. **Is the vulnerability authentic?** "Aww thanks" is performative. "I feel nothing" or "I don't even know what I'm doing with my life" is raw.

3. **Does the pacing reflect emotional processing?** Real people pause. They send "..." They type and delete. They struggle with words.

4. **Is the emotional payoff earned?** The resolution should feel like a release of built tension, not a forced lesson.

5. **Are the delays realistic?** 
   - Quick back-and-forth during casual chat: 800-1200ms
   - Hesitation before vulnerability: 2000-3000ms
   - Processing hard truth: 1500-2500ms
   - Excited rapid-fire: 600-800ms

## 7. INMYHEADTEXTS SIGNATURE MOVES

**The "K" Weapon:**
- Single letter "K" or "👍" = shutting down
- Partner refuses to accept it: "idc how much we argue you still my baby"
- Resolution: commitment through resistance

**The "..." Struggle:**
- Shows someone typing, stopping, thinking
- Indicates emotional difficulty
- Often appears before vulnerable admissions

**The Caps Lock Moment:**
- Always means emotional peak
- Excitement: "I'M SCREAMING", "AAAAAA"
- Anger: "WHAT", "STOP"
- Desperation: "WHY", "PLEASE"

**The Paragraph Bomb:**
- After many short messages, one person unloads
- Shows accumulated pain or thought
- Forces confrontation or clarity

**The Pet Name Escalation:**
- "baby" → "wife/husband"
- "babe" → "my [noun]"
- The upgrade causes emotional reaction

**The "Ohh" Realization:**
- Single "Ohh" or "Oh." or "oh..."
- Shows understanding, often painful
- Signals acceptance of truth
